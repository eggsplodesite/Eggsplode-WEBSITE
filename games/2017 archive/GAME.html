Description

Apocalypse Rising is an open world zombie survival game where the zombies aren't the only threat. Group up, repair vehicles, build bases, eliminate the infected, and watch out for hostile survivors!

A weld issue related to firearms and melee weapons was fixed in the latest update.

VIP servers are now only 100 R$ per month. You can subscribe to a private VIP server by clicking on the "Servers" tab. VIP servers allow owners to kick and ban players, as well as set moderators who can do the same. A variety of settings are available - from spawning items, to creating boarder walls, to spectating other players. VIP servers are perfect for hosting tournaments or just playing with your friends. The owner can access these features by clicking the "+" icon in the bottom-left corner of their screen once they join their VIP server and select a map. Check them out!

Apocalypse Rising does not support mobile platforms. We apologize for the inconvenience.
